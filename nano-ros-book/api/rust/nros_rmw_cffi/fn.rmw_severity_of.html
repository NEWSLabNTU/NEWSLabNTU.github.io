Map nros_log’s ladder onto upstream’s.

Trace has no upstream counterpart and folds into DEBUG. Losing a distinction upstream never had is better than inventing a value a ROS-side caller could not produce — the mapping is deliberately lossy in the direction that keeps the wire vocabulary standard.
