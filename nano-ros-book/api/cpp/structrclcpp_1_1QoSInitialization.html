Detailed Description

Upstream's QoSInitialization (rclcpp/qos.hpp): the history policy and depth a QoS is built from. ADOPT-BOUNDED: upstream's KeepLast and KeepAll derive from it, and ours are functions returning a whole QoS (see KeepLast above), so this converts implicitly from a QoS instead, taking its history and depth — rclcpp::QoS(rclcpp::KeepLast(1), profile) compiles either way.
